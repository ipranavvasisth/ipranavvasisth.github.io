About

I am a mechanical engineering undergraduate at the Bangalore Institute of Technology, driven by a deep fascination for aerospace engineering and the physics of flight. My academic journey is defined by a desire to understand how things move through air and space, and how computational tools can help us understand the physics behind them and how we can improve them.

Beyond core engineering, I have cultivated a growing proficiency in Python and machine learning, applying computational thinking to engineering problems. I believe the future of aerospace lies at the intersection of classical mechanics and data-driven approaches, and that is precisely the space I want to occupy.
