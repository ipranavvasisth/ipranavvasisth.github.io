Hey everyone! I'm Pranav Vasisth, and welcome to my very first blog post. 

I'm currently an undergraduate student at the Bangalore Institute of Technology (BIT), pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering (Class of 2029). While my degree says "Mechanical," my true passion lies at the intersection of physical hardware, software, and aerodynamics.

### What Drives Me
From the start of my engineering journey, I've been fascinated by how we can blend traditional mechanical principles with modern technology. I spend a lot of my time diving into Aerospace Engineering, specifically focusing on aircraft engines, aerodynamics, and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD). 

### Beyond the Screen
Engineering is about discipline, and I've started applying that same mindset to my personal health. Since early May, I've been on a structured fitness journey, hitting the gym to build strength and manage my weight.

### What to Expect Here
I'm starting this blog to document my journey. Expect to see deep dives into cool concepts, my learning experiences. 

Thanks for stopping by. Let's build something awesome.
